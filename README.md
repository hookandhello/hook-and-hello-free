# Hook and Hello Free

Is your hook ready to post? Free Claude skills for a one-person business that sells on Instagram.

- **Hook critic lite** (in this repo): paste a reel hook, a script or a caption and get PASS or REVISE with the main reason, in plain words. See [skills/hook-critic-lite](skills/hook-critic-lite/).
- **Caption writer** (free download): paste a reel script or a topic and get a short caption with your call to action on line 1, plus a first comment.

Both skills write drafts only. Nothing is posted, sent or deleted: you read it, change what you like, and post it yourself.

## Example

On a made-up watercolour class business:

    Hook: "Granulation versus blooms but make it wet-on-damp energy."
    VERDICT: REVISE
    WHY: Your audience is returning beginners who want a calm hobby back, and "granulation", "blooms" and "wet-on-damp energy" are jargon they would have to decode. It names a topic rather than something they want to be able to do.
